Skippy Voice, ElevenLabs Print

The local engines now clone zero-shot from a 9-second clip of ElevenLabs' own Skippy voice (the designed "Arrogant Asshole", the one the chat plays), three settings each. Every take, ElevenLabs included, has been through the chat's own polish chain (RNNoise and the warmth EQ) and has the same loudness.

Averages over 3 lines. RTF is synthesis time over audio length (below 1 is faster than real time). The picture studio was using the GPU during these runs, so the local timings are slower than on a quiet card (on a quiet card F5 was about 2 s a line). Like ElevenLabs is the cosine between voice prints (Resemblyzer): about 0.8 and up usually means the same voice. Pause noise is the level in the quiet bits, dBFS: lower is cleaner.
