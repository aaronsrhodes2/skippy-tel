Skippy Voice Bake-off

Each line below is spoken six ways: by two ElevenLabs voices (the designed Skippy voice in the vault, and the voice the chat's speaker key actually plays today) and by four local models on skippy-pc's GPU, each cloning zero-shot from the same 11-second clip of the source recording below. Letters are shuffled on every line, and every clip has the same loudness and codec.

Play the takes, give each one 1 to 5 stars for how much it sounds like Skippy, and tick the one you think is ElevenLabs. Then press Reveal. Your ratings stay in this browser.
