Averages over the 3 lines. RTF is synthesis time divided by audio length: below 1 is faster than real time. First audio is the first streamed chunk where the engine streams (ElevenLabs, XTTS), otherwise the whole line. VRAM is the model's own peak; "whole card in use" is the card's peak during the run, everything included (Ollama, the picture studio and the desktop share the same 16 GB). The picture studio was generating images during the Chatterbox and Qwen3 runs (GPU at 100 %), so those two are slower than on a quiet card; "RTF, first run" is the same three lines from the 2026-10-20 run, with only Ollama's model on the card.
