## Start from v2, don't start over
build.json is your v2 build (git history has v1). Its parts are good: the boiler, domes, bell, cab, cowcatcher, smoke, the
passenger cars with clerestory roofs and windows, the platform, depot and water tower. Keep them; fix what is wrong.

## What v2 got wrong (the runtime proves it)
`node minebench_runtime.mjs build.json` now reports FLOATING PARTS: pieces of blocks joined face to face that don't reach the
ground. On v2 it reports 123 floating pieces, 25,705 blocks:
- the whole locomotive with its smoke (12,846 blocks, x 140-210): it rests on nothing, so the boiler and frame do not touch the
  wheels, and the wheels do not stand on the rails;
- both passenger cars (4,596 blocks each, z 23-36 and z 70-83) and their roofs (1,544 each): the cars are not on the track at all
  (the track is at z 124-132); they float beside the plate;
- small pieces: railings, posts and trim that don't touch what they belong to.
The vision model looked at v2 and called it "recognisable", and missed all of this. Describing a picture is not checking it.

## The checks, every pass
1. Run the runtime. `floating_parts` must be 0 except the smoke plume (one piece, white_wool/gray_wool, above the stack). For each
   other floating piece it prints the box and materials: find the code that made it and fix the coordinates so it touches what
   holds it up (wheels on rails, frame on wheels, body on frame, roof on walls, railings on platforms). Run again until clean.
2. Render: `python D:/Aaron/development/SkippyTel/tools/voxel/render.py build.json shots/v3-passN.png "v3 pass N" --zoom 0.6`.
3. Look, with pointed questions. Read the picture and check, one by one, against what it describes:
   - Are both passenger cars ON the track, in line behind the tender, nose to tail, and not beside it?
   - Are the wheels vertical discs standing on the rails, under the locomotive, tender and cars, and not lying flat or apart?
   - Is the locomotive's body joined to its frame and wheels: no gap between the boiler and the wheels?
   - Is the smokestack the tallest part, flared, with the smoke rising from it and drifting back?
   - Can you see the cab's windows, the domes and the bell, and the cowcatcher at the very front?
   If the description doesn't answer a question, or answers it wrong, fix that and look again.
4. At least three passes. Stop only when the runtime is clean (smoke excepted) AND every question above is answered yes.
