Maker Portal

Maker Portal (makerportal.live) is a private home portal run by one person, {{ switch_owner }}, for himself and a few friends. It is not a commercial service and has no ads.

What it does

  • Runs and shows the status of a few private game servers for friends.
  • Plays his own music, audiobook and video collections in the browser.
  • Skippy, a home assistant (chat and voice), answers questions and runs his house: lights, music, and a morning and bedtime summary.
  • Public experiments: physics demos anyone can open without signing in (Experiments).

Signing in with Google

Sign-in uses Google only to learn your email address, so the portal knows whether you are on the list. Nothing else of yours is read.

Google data, for the owner only

The owner, and only the owner, can choose to connect his own Google account so Skippy can read, read only, his calendar, Gmail, contacts and Google Tasks. Skippy uses them for one thing: telling him about his day in the morning and bedtime routines (today's events, bills and mail from people he knows, packages arriving, birthdays, to-dos). It never sends, changes or deletes anything, never shares or sells the data, never shows it to anyone else, and never uses it for advertising, to train AI models, or to make images. The summaries are written by a model running on his own computers. He can disconnect at any time, which revokes the access at Google.

Maker Portal's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
