Improvement distillation

A branch that runs every day: purple Skippy writes a new set of short science questions of the kind Wolfram Alpha's free tier accepts, with our own oracle answer where we have one. Each goes to Wolfram Alpha once and to Mentat. Mentat is judged against our oracle, and a miss is classified from Mentat's own record (no route, bad arguments, tool error, wrong tool, wrong value). Wolfram's column is the yardstick only: nothing it answers is used to improve Mentat; our own questions, answers and miss reasons are.
