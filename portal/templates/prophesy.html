Shifting Prophesy: purple Skippy's shake-down cruise

Purple Skippy's shake-down cruise. The home AI, running as itself in the workshop on Skippy Core's local model, builds a whole browser game: a top-down adventure with a procedurally generated overworld and dungeons, from a written spec, one prompt from the Captain at a time. Claude Haiku gets the same prompt with every tool Claude Code has, as the reference. A referee checks both builds against the spec on 500 seeds neither can see; purple tries each turn again, from scratch, until it matches what Haiku made of it. One page per turn (flip with a swipe or the arrow keys); on a turn's page, every attempt is a section that opens on its own: model:turn:attempt, so purple:1:7 is purple's seventh try at turn 1.
